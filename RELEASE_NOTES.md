First Apple Silicon preview of **Xodus Nexus Neo**, an independent fork of [Xodus Nexus](https://github.com/domsvoboda/xodus-nexus).

## Included

- Xbox Series X|S console Remote Play and Xbox Cloud Gaming.
- Native macOS controller input, including Bluetooth Nintendo Switch Pro controllers.
- Batched cloud catalog loading, console-generation/genre/publisher/play-style filters and date sorting.
- Clickable Store details and screenshot galleries.
- Xbox Recently Played history independent of installed downloads.
- Encrypted catalog and history caching across restarts.
- Optional Discord Rich Presence configured with your public Discord application ID.

## Install

Requires **Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or newer**. Download the ARM64 DMG, open it and drag Xodus Nexus Neo to Applications, or extract the ZIP. SHA-256 checksums are included in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

This preview uses a local ad-hoc signature and is **not Developer ID signed or notarized**. macOS may require you to approve opening it. Existing preview sign-ins, settings and browsing caches use the same internal profile identity.

## Limitations

- Local Windows PC game execution is not implemented on macOS; use console or cloud streaming to play.
- Streaming availability follows your account, region, subscription and Xbox services.
- Microphone chat and controller vibration are not included.
- Discord is off by default and requires your own public application ID. Console activity identifies the console; cloud and local activity can identify the game.

Original MIT license and third-party attribution are retained. Report Neo issues in this repository, rather than the upstream project.
